Parameter Tuning Guide

Mode:
- Spline: Bezier curves, good for anime/lineart.
- Fourier: Epicycles, good for continuous loops.
- Chaikin: Polylines with corner cutting.

Detail (Tolerance):
Higher = More equations, tighter fit to original shape. Lower = abstract, smaller AST.

Min Path:
Higher = Filters out tiny noise lines and compression artifacts. Keeps output clean.

🔥 Recommended Combos:

Anime / Lineart: Spline | Detail 80 | Min Path 10
Smooth, flowing hair lines.

Logos / Geo: Spline | Detail 90 | Min Path 5
Sharp corners, precise geometry.

Landscapes: Spline | Detail 30 | Min Path 20
Abstract, stylized, prevents bloat.

Noisy JPEG: Spline | Detail 50 | Min Path 15
Heals compression damage.
