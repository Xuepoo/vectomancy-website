Video Engine Guide

Modes:

  • Spline: Bezier curves. Best for lineart.
  • Fourier: Epicycle loops. Chaotic aesthetic.
  • Chaikin: Smooth polylines. Hand-drawn look.

Parameters:

  • Detail: 10-100. Lower = abstract, Higher = precise.
  • Min Path: Filters noise. Clean = 5, Noisy = 15+.
  • Terms: Fourier complexity.
  • Iterations: Chaikin smoothing depth.

Export:

  • Use CLI for full video export: vectomancy video input.mp4 -o output.mp4
  • Web preview uses WASM (slower, frame-by-frame)
